Follow one company

This is the rare sector where the cohort is the industry: all four milling companies licensed to process the Kingdom's subsidized wheat are listed, all four report to 31 December, and all four are covered here. A “sector median” on this site is therefore the median of the whole industry rather than of a sample. The histories are not equally long: the companies were carved out of the state grains organization and sold in 2020 and 2021, and the earliest years on each sheet are pre-privatization carve-out accounts, which is why several series begin part way across the period.
